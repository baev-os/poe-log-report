Each map entry appears as a separate row, from earlier to later events.
Returning to the same map remains a separate visit. A confirmed common part
across multiple files counts once even if their continuations diverge.
A shared entry remains one visit; expand its branches to see their own
boundaries and evidence. Measured branch durations are added after subtracting
the shared part. Matching times after divergence do not prove duplication.
An unknown exit is not inferred; known durations from other branches remain
in the total, marked as partially measured.

Visits are ordered by entry time, or by exit time when entry is unknown.
Only when both times are missing are log anchors and neighbouring records
used; this placement is approximate. Without a time anchor, only the record
sequence is known. Missing times are not filled in, and duration is not
calculated when a boundary is unknown.

Visits are grouped by month and day in the report's time zone.
The group date is the entry date, or the exit date when entry is unknown.
A visit crossing midnight remains one row with its full duration on the
entry day; these are visit totals, not time split across calendar days.
Records with neither timestamp appear separately under “Unknown date”.
Select a date to collapse or expand its table.

Mechanic tags come from recognized NPC dialogue during a map visit:
Delirium, Blight, Harvest, Incursion, Bestiary, Delve, Betrayal, Ultimatum,
Maven and Expedition. Repeated dialogue produces one tag per visit;
select it to open the first supporting line. A tag records dialogue,
not encounter counts, successful completions or rewards. Missing dialogue
does not prove a mechanic was absent. Hideouts and standalone arenas
do not add tags to neighbouring maps.

Expand death messages to see the character, time and original line.
Messages may refer to party members, so they are not treated as your
personal death count. The `/deaths` counter is not added to this total.

Separate tags show notifications for Nameless Seer, Reflecting Mist and
a discoverable hideout. They do not confirm a purchase, reward or hideout
unlock. A hideout notification after generation but before entry belongs
to the new map. Repeated notifications within a visit produce one tag
linked to the first message. Death from self-damage is labelled separately.

“Mercenary greetings” expands to show the name, time and original greeting
with a link to the log history. Known names from the Cyaxan, Keitan, Azadin
and Bardiyan families must match a specific greeting; a companion's combat
dialogue alone is not enough. Repeated names keep the first greeting per
visit. Returning to a map starts a separate list. These records do not prove
victory, recruitment or rarity and are not a count of unique encounters.
