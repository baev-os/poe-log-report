Duration is measured time on the map, excluding gaps between visits.
The map belongs to the day of its first entry. A dash means no duration was measured.
If some visits are incomplete, their known portions contribute to the row and daily
total, marked “partially measured”. Unknown entry or exit times remain dashes.

Maps are ordered by entry time; an unknown exit does not affect their position.
If entry is unknown, exit time is used for ordering. If both are missing,
an approximate anchor from the log is used. Maps without a first entry date
appear in “Entry date unknown” and in the map stream.
Neighbouring timestamps are never presented as the entry time.
