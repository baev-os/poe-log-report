# Game materials

Path of Exile map images, area names, identifiers, and dialogue
belong to Grinding Gear Games. This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by the company.
The project's MIT license covers its own code, not game materials.

## Maintainer decision

On 2026-10-29, the maintainer decided to retain the included game materials in
good faith for this fan-made, noncommercial log-analysis project. The maintainer
refers to fair use as the project's rationale. That rationale is not an
established legal determination, confirmation of an applicable exception, or
permission from Grinding Gear Games. No written GGG redistribution approval
is recorded in the project.

Grinding Gear Games retains its rights and may request removal of its materials.
The maintainer will promptly remove or replace affected materials on a request
from GGG, including in the source and downloads controlled by the project.
Sources, hashes, and required third-party notices will be kept accurate when
materials change.

The project's noncommercial purpose does not add restrictions to the MIT license
for its original code. It does not grant additional rights to game materials.
